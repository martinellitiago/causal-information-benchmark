Omega D generators
==================

Generate a known mechanism, sample it reproducibly, and inspect where its
information becomes accessible under conditioning.

This first release implements **discrete IID distributions** and **four-node
binary lag-one processes**. Continuous and mixed-state models are planned.
The simplex figures remain a separate illustration of joint probability laws.
